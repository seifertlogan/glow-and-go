Full screen now

Every touch lights something, wherever it lands — no aiming, scores, timers or failure. Lights fade in and out slowly; there is no flashing or strobing. Sound and music are off unless you turn them on, are always quiet, and fade in slowly. Music starts at the child's first touch. If you hear nothing, check the iPad's side mute switch and volume. Works offline once installed. Settings are saved on this device only. To exit full screen, press and hold the ⚙ button, or use the device's back/home gesture. If the child seems uncomfortable, try “Calm & soft”, lower the brightness, or take a break.
